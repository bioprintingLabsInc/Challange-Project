GSE275931 — Dolutegravir-Exposed Human Forebrain Organoids

GEO Accession: GSE275931

GEO Link: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE275931

Dataset Category: DNT / chemical-exposed brain organoid RNA-seq

Organism: Homo sapiens

Model: Human iPSC-derived forebrain organoids

RNA Sequencing: Bulk RNA-seq

iPSC Line: C1-2

Compound: Dolutegravir (DTG)

Concentration: 10 µM

Exposure Duration: 12 days

Experimental Comparison: 10 µM dolutegravir-treated forebrain organoids versus DMSO vehicle-treated control organoids.

Samples
	•	3 dolutegravir-treated samples
	•	3 DMSO vehicle-control samples
	•	6 total bulk RNA-seq samples

Control: DMSO was used as the vehicle control.

DNT Classification: Dolutegravir is currently classified in this repository as a test compound.

The DMSO vehicle control should not be interpreted as a DNT-negative reference 
compound.

This dataset does not contain a separate established DNT-negative compound.

Repository Contents

processed/
Processed bulk RNA-seq expression files downloaded from GEO.

metadata/
SRARunTable.csv

Original sequencing-run metadata downloaded from the NCBI SRA Run Selector.

samples_to_use.csv

Curated project metadata describing the six samples included in this dataset, 
including treatment, concentration, exposure duration, iPSC line, sample type, and 
DNT classification.

Raw Sequencing Data

Raw sequencing reads are available through NCBI SRA and are not stored directly in this GitHub repository.

Intended Project Use

Chronic chemical-exposure human brain organoid dataset for development and evaluation of the ML-based transcriptomic developmental neurotoxicity pipeline.

Important Notes
	•	Human forebrain organoids
	•	Bulk RNA-seq
	•	10 µM dolutegravir exposure
	•	12-day exposure duration
	•	C1-2 iPSC line
	•	3 treated and 3 vehicle-control samples
	•	Meets the project's minimum one-week exposure requirement
	•	Does not contain a dedicated DNT-negative reference compound




GSE210682 — Methadone-Exposed Human Cortical Organoids

GEO Accession: GSE210682

GEO Link: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE210682

Dataset Category: DNT / chemical-exposed brain organoid RNA-seq

Organism: Homo sapiens

Model: Human iPSC-derived cortical organoids

RNA Sequencing: Bulk RNA-seq

iPSC Lines: Cell Line A and Cell Line B

iPSC Origin: iPSC lines were derived from fibroblasts obtained from two healthy adult male donors.

Compound: Methadone

Concentration: 1 µM

Exposure Duration: 50 days

Experimental Timeline

Methadone treatment began during cortical organoid development and continued until RNA collection at Day 60.

Experimental Comparison

1 µM methadone-treated cortical organoids versus vehicle-treated control organoids.
Samples
	•	7 methadone-treated samples
	•	7 vehicle-control samples
	•	14 total bulk RNA-seq samples

Cell Line A
	•	3 methadone-treated samples
	•	3 vehicle-control samples

Cell Line B
	•	4 methadone-treated samples
	•	4 vehicle-control samples

Control

Nuclease-free water was used as the vehicle control.

DNT Classification: Methadone is currently classified in this repository as a test compound.

The vehicle control should not be interpreted as a DNT-negative reference compound.
This dataset does not contain a separate established DNT-negative compound.
